# Privacy Policy - WhatsApp Emoji Reaction Customizer

**Last Updated: February 22, 2026**

## Overview

WhatsApp Emoji Reaction Customizer ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect
- **Emoji preferences**: The custom emojis you select are stored using Chrome's sync storage API.

### What We Do NOT Collect
- Personal information
- Browsing history
- WhatsApp messages or conversations
- Contact information
- Any data from WhatsApp Web

## Data Storage

- All data is stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.sync`
- If you are signed into Chrome, your emoji preferences sync across your devices through Google's infrastructure
- We do not have access to this data
- We do not operate any servers that collect or store your data

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data to third parties.

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `storage` | Save your emoji preferences locally |
| `activeTab` | Access the current WhatsApp Web tab to modify the reaction menu |
| `host_permissions` (web.whatsapp.com) | Required to inject content script only on WhatsApp Web |

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please create an issue on our GitHub repository.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Clear your stored emoji preferences at any time via the extension popup ("Reset" button)
- Uninstall the extension to remove all stored data
- Disable Chrome sync to prevent cross-device synchronization
